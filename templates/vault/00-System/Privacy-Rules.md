# Privacy Rules

## Normal

General notes, public sources, ordinary tasks, and non-sensitive project context.

## Sensitive

Health, personal contact details, private relationship details, work-sensitive context, and recovery anchors. Preserve originals, but minimize duplication in compiled notes.

## Secret

Passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, recovery codes, private keys, financial account numbers, and government identification numbers.

Do not copy secrets into review notes, Library notes, Projects, Relationships, Tasks, indexes, or the Change Log.

Store secrets in a password manager or OS-backed secret store.
